Find answers to common questions about Ogalab

Ogalab is a laboratory and diagnostic center management system for handling patient records, laboratory tests, samples, lab results, billing, referrals, scan workflows and reporting, analytics, administrative control and more.

Ogalab is designed for:

  • Diagnostic laboratories
  • Medical testing centers
  • Clinics with laboratory services
  • Facilities that also manage ultrasound scan workflows

Ogalab includes patient management, sample tracking, test catalog and result management, billing and payments, referral tracking, ultrasound scan reporting, customizable report formats, analytics dashboards, and secure role-based access control.
